Ingredients

  • 5 lemons
  • 5 cups water
  • 1 cup sugar

Equipment

  • Measuring cup
  • Jar
  • Lemon squeezer
  • Large spoon

Directions

  1. Pour 5 cups of water into jar
  2. Add 1 cup of sugar and mix until fully dissolved
  3. Squeeze 5 lemons into jar and mix
  4. Add ice if desired and serve!
